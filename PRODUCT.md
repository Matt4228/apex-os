# Apex OS

## What it is
A to-do app with long-term tracking, used top down:
1. Define the goals and habits you want.
2. Build a weekly schedule around your appointments, with work blocks for getting things done.
3. Create tasks from your goals, habits, schedule and competitions. Standalone tasks are allowed, but tasks that come from something bigger are the point.
4. Compete with friends toward shared goals.

The home page answers one question: what should I do today?

## Who it's for
Portfolio first: recruiters and engineers clicking through the demo for a few minutes.
Built like a real tool: I use it daily, so it has to hold up beyond the first impression.

## Register
Product UI. The design serves the task. Familiar patterns over novelty.

## Feel
Calm, focused, a little cinematic. A tool that disappears while you work,
with moments of satisfaction when you finish something.

## References
- **Obsidian:** dark, quiet surfaces; a purple accent used sparingly; side navigation; command palette.
- **VS Code:** density without clutter; an activity bar plus sidebar; everything reachable from the keyboard.
- **Netflix:** content first; the most relevant thing ("continue watching") leads; dark backdrop that lets color pop.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: rows of identical stat tiles, every section in a bordered card.
- Loud color everywhere; gradients and glass for decoration.

## Theme
Light by default, with a dark option.

## Principles
1. Structure should be felt, not seen: hierarchy from type and spacing before boxes and borders.
2. Today first: what you need now leads, history and settings come after.
3. Color means something: purple for brand and current location, teal for actions, red only for danger.
4. Every text color passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes.
5. Tasks come from somewhere: every feature should help turn goals and the schedule into today's tasks.
